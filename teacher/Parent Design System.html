Phase 5 · Ota-ona portali

Your child is in good hands.

The Parent portal is a quiet, warm window into the school day — built mobile-first for tired-but-hopeful parents who check it on the bus, in the kitchen, between meetings. It uses twilight blue on warm cream paper, with each day rendered as a stitched page in a book — a small keepsake, not a feed.

Brand-600 background, white initials. Parent-side fallback is brand-colored (not child-ribbon) — the one child IS the surface.

A parent should be able to read a day card even if no photo loaded. Text always carries the meaning; photos add warmth. Day cards never lead with a full-bleed photo — the date and title come first, the way a letter would.

Privacy & consent

Only your child

Parents see only photos of their own child. Group shots where other children appear are filtered server-side. If a photo can't be safely shared, the day card silently shows fewer photos — never an error.

Save & share

Parents can save photos and entire day cards as keepsakes. Sharing outside the app prompts a calm reminder about other families' privacy — not a blocking warning.

05

When the news is sensitive

Behavior incidents, missed milestones, illness — these can frighten a parent. Every sensitive notice follows the same four-part shape: context · what happened · next step · call teacher.

Eslatma · 14 mart

Yusuf bugun ozgina bezovta edi

Erkin o'yin paytida baland ovozdan biroz qiynaldi. Dilnoza opa uni jim joyga olib chiqdi, suv ichirdi va birgalikda 10 daqiqa o'tirdi. Soat 11:30 ga kelib u o'zini yaxshi his qildi va boshqa bolalar bilan o'ynashda davom etdi.

Kontekst

Bu Yusufning birinchi marta tinch joyga o'tishi emas — biz buni ish rejasida belgilab qo'yganmiz. Bunday holatlar uning baland ovozli muhitga moslashuvining bir qismi.

Keyingi qadam

Ertaga ham xuddi shu vaziyatni kuzatamiz. Agar uyda quloqqa teginib bezovta bo'lishi davom etsa, logoped bilan kichik ko'rik tashkil qilamiz.

Savollaringiz bo'lsa, qo'ng'iroq qiling yoki yozing. Bu butun kun ish vaqtida mumkin.

Same shape, three intensities — warning (above), info (illness/absence), error (only for safety incidents requiring immediate response).

Anatomy
  1. 1
    Eslatma label — semantic tag with date. Always the first thing the parent sees.
  2. 2
    Headline — calm declarative sentence. No exclamation. No "incident" or "alert".
  3. 3
    What happened — short paragraph, named action by the teacher, named outcome. End on something the child did well or recovered to.
  4. 4
    Kontekst — frames the event. "This is normal / expected / planned for."
  5. 5
    Keyingi qadam — what the school will do. Action belongs with the school, not loaded onto the parent.
  6. 6
    Call teacher — primary action. Always reachable in one tap.
Copy rules
"Yusuf bugun ozgina bezovta edi."
"Yusuf jiddiy buzilish ko'rsatdi!"
Active voice. Named teacher. Named outcome.
Passive voice. "Was observed". Open-ended worry.
Call-teacher button

Direct dial during school hours (08:00–16:00). Outside those hours, the same button becomes "Xabar yozish" as primary — the parent never feels stuck.

Notes
  • Phone-first auth (no email). The school already has the parent's number.

First thing the parent sees
  1. Child switcher — even with one child, it's there as a confidence signal.
  2. Greeting line — date + author + send time, in serif. Sets the "letter" feel before any card opens.
  3. Today's card — full body, full photos, milestone sticker if any.
  4. Stitched boundary — "avvalgi kunlar" label. Today never sits in the same flow as yesterday.
  5. Recent days — compact cards, scrollable.
No empty home

If today has no entry yet, the top card is a quiet "Bugun hali yozuv yo'q" with a small calendar icon — never a loading skeleton, never an apology. The "avvalgi kunlar" stack still loads underneath.

Pull-to-refresh

Pull reveals a single serif line: "Yangilanmoqda…" No spinning circles in the body. The list updates in place.

Goals · parent-friendly translation

"IEP Goal #3: Fine-motor scissor manipulation along linear axis" becomes "Qaychi bilan kesish · to'g'ri chiziq bo'ylab". No clinical codes on this surface. Teacher controls phrasing; backend stores both.

Progress bars · soft, not stressful
  • Honey-500 fill for "approaching mastery" (60%+).
  • Brand-500 for in-progress (5–60%).
  • No red. Slow goals don't get punished by color.
  • A short qualitative line accompanies every number — context, not just data.
Mastered goals stay visible

Once achieved, a goal doesn't disappear — it becomes a honey-tinted card the parent can revisit. The book metaphor again: pages turned, not erased.

Multi-child

Profile is always one child. The switcher at the top is the only way to change context — never inline. Diyora's profile is its own surface with its own teacher card and goals.

Closing note

A small window, not a feed

Parents check Uchqun on the bus, between meetings, before bed. The portal should leave them feeling reassured — not informed-and-alarmed. Every surface above respects the parent's adulthood, their time, and their child's dignity.
